TOURISM IN AUSTRALIA

Impact of COVID-19 on Tourism

In this line plot, there is an increasing trend in the number of arrivals and departures to and from Australia. There is also a structural break at the beginning of 2020, where the arrivals and departures dropped significantly. This is because Australia shut their borders at this time, so that no one could enter or exit the country. The borders eventually reopened early in 2022, which is when the arrivals and departures increased and returned close to their pre-COVID numbers.

In this stacked bar chart, most countries experience a decrease in tourists coming to Australia from 2019 to 2024. The country with the largest drop off of visitors is China, with a 49% decrease in tourists coming to Australia between these two time periods. The main reason for this drop off is likely COVID-19, which caused travelling in general to decrease.

Visitor Demographics

In this treemap, major countries that are geographically close to Australia have lots of tourists going to Australia, such as New Zealand and China. Conversely, countries that are further away from Australia tend to have less tourists coming to Australia, such as Netherlands, Italy, and other European countries. This is because there are less direct flights from these countries to Australia, due to the distance.

In this bar chart, the two main reasons for tourists coming to Australia are for a holiday and to visit friends/relatives, with 40.7% and 35.3% respectively stating these reasons. Australia is a popular holiday destination, because there are nice beaches, warm weather and beautiful landscapes. These features also make Australia an ideal spot for retirement, so young people may visit their retired relatives when they move to Australia.
